# Pricing & Packaging Information

## Pricing Framework
- Entry range: ₹199 - ₹299
- Mid range: ₹349 - ₹499
- Premium sets: ₹699+

## Packaging Standards
- Use recyclable containers where possible.
- Include batch no., mfg date, expiry/best-before, net volume/weight.
- Add usage instructions and patch-test advisory for topical products.
