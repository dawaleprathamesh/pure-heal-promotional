# Marketing Strategy

## Positioning
Natural daily care for conscious consumers seeking effective, gentle products.

## Target Audience
- Women and men 20-45
- Wellness-focused buyers
- Ingredient-conscious shoppers

## Channel Mix
- Instagram + Facebook for awareness
- WhatsApp + email for conversion/retention
- LinkedIn for partnerships and credibility

## 90-Day Focus
1. Build product education content.
2. Run starter acquisition offers.
3. Capture testimonials and UGC.
4. Track CAC, conversion, repeat purchase.
