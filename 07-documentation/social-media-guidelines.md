# Social Media Guidelines

## Posting Cadence
- Instagram: 5 posts/week + stories daily
- Facebook: 3-4 posts/week
- LinkedIn: 2 posts/week

## Creative Rules
- Keep visuals natural, clean, and ingredient-led.
- Ensure claims are realistic and compliant.
- Include CTA in every post.

## Engagement SLA
- Reply to comments/messages within 24 hours.
