# Partner & Influencer Collaboration Guidelines

## Partner Fit Criteria
- Natural wellness alignment
- Authentic audience engagement
- Prior skincare/haircare relevance

## Deliverables Template
- 1 Reel + 2 Stories + 1 static post
- Mandatory product mention and usage disclosure
- 7-day post-live analytics share

## Compliance
- Include paid collaboration disclosure when applicable.
- Avoid unsupported medical claims.
