# PureHeal Brand Guidelines

## Color Palette
- Primary: `#DCEFF2` (Soft Mint Blue)
- Secondary: `#1C2A2E` (Deep Botanical)
- Accent: `#6E8B6A` (Leaf Green)
- Neutral: `#F7F7F2` (Natural White)

## Typography
- Heading: Serif (e.g., Playfair Display / Cormorant)
- Body: Sans-serif (e.g., Lato / Open Sans)
- Accent script: Handwritten style for benefit highlights only

## Brand Voice
- Tone: Calm, trustworthy, natural, expert-friendly
- Messaging style: Benefit-led, transparent, gentle claims
- Avoid: Medical promises or absolute cure language
