# PureHeal Marketing & Promotional Repository

This repository is the central collaboration space for PureHeal marketing.

## Brand Overview
PureHeal is a natural wellness brand offering oils, facepacks, and scrubs made with nature-first ingredients.

## Repository Structure
- `01-brand-assets/` - brand guidelines, logo usage, and sticker references
- `02-product-catalogs/` - oils, facepacks, scrubs, pricing, packaging
- `03-marketing-materials/` - social, email, landing page, and press templates
- `04-content-strategy/` - calendar, blog ideas, testimonials, hashtags
- `05-campaign-assets/` - promotional graphics, banners, and social post templates
- `06-analytics-tracking/` - campaign and social metrics templates
- `07-documentation/` - strategy and execution guidelines
- `08-resources/` - tools, vendors, and media kit template

2. Fill campaign/product-specific details.

4. Track outcomes in analytics templates.
